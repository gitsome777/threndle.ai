The threndle.ai diagnostic: three minutes to a straight answer

The diagnostic is a short questionnaire that figures out which operational bottleneck an AI implementation should fix first. It asks what is actually eating your week across money, sales, marketing, customer service, admin, and hiring, which tools you already use, how many hours manual work is costing you, and what budget and urgency sit behind fixing it.

What you get

Your answers are scored against urgency, hours lost, and budget, and you get a straight recommendation, not a packaged sales pitch:

  • Foundation: one bottleneck solved, fixed price, typically $3,000 to $3,500.
  • Growth: a multi-workflow build, $6,000 to $9,000 fixed fee over 4 to 6 weeks.
  • Partner: an ongoing retainer, $750 to $1,500 per month, for businesses that need continuous tuning.

What happens next

We confirm the scope on a short call before anything is signed. Then we connect your tools and deliver the first workflow live against your real data, not a demo. You are trained on it, checked in on at 30 days, and can scale up whenever you are ready.
